mosi was built with mentorship from dr. michael drebot, former director of zoonotic diseases at canada's national microbiology lab. the validation folder reruns the scoring against real west nile surveillance records to see if the numbers hold up. it won a manitoba schools science symposium silver medal and the canadian meteorological and oceanographic society award for environmental science. It was presented to the Minister of Innovation and is in concurrent development set for launch soon.

cleanit came out of a canu and kahanee initiative to spread compassion in Winnipeg, it has raised over $2.5k in funding and has been presented to the mayor of winnipeg. it is built around wahkohtowin, the cree understanding that all living things are related and that being related carries responsibility.
